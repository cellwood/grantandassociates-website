# Insights

Tips, advice and insights for executives and leaders about how to elevate their speaking skills, including presentation skills, media skills, and public speaking skills. In the boardroom, at the roundtable, or in the media, what you say and how you say it will have a big impact on your success. Judy Grant shares insights garnered from decades of experience as a TV anchor, reporter and producer and as a professional media and presentation coach working with C-suite executives, celebrities and leaders. She uses examples from popular culture, news and events, and personal stories to illustrate the power and impact of good (and bad) speaking skills. Her passion is to help people to raise their voices and change the world.

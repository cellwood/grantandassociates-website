# Privacy Policy

This is the website of Grant and Associates.

We collect the name, email address, organization and message you choose to send us through our contact form, so that we can reply to you. The form is delivered to us by email through a form service. We do not sell or share your contact information with third parties.

If you would like us to delete your information, or you do not want to receive email from us in the future, please let us know through the [contact form](/contact/).

This policy is subject to change without notice.
